TONES

All 8 tones sit within 1884-3445 Hz (one octave), so no harmonic hits another tone. Intermod products stay 29 Hz or more from every tone.

SYMBOL 1 symbol = 1 byte (ASCII | 0x80). Tones for 1-bits sound together. bit7 is the pilot, always on: it marks symbol timing, even for NUL. 5 ms fade, silent gap between symbols.

FRAME DEL DEL STX body ETX sum

DEL (0x7F) sounds every tone: the receiver calibrates per-tone response from it. Body is plain ASCII (LF and TAB ok, other chars sent as ?). sum = body bytes mod 128.

RECEIVE Pilot onset finds each symbol, then Hann window + Goertzel measures 8 tones mid-symbol. A tone is 1 when about -3.5 dB or more against the pilot (after DEL calibration).
